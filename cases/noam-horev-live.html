NOAM HOREV — LIVE

Summary

The new touring concert season of Noam Horev — the ACUM Prize-winning lyricist, poet and author whose songs have been performed by Israel's leading artists, and whose solo show has run more than 1,200 performances over the past seven years. In the new production, for the first time, Horev takes the stage with a full live band and guest vocalists — Yossi Ram, Itay Paz and Stav Vaknin — performing the hits he wrote, turning an intimate evening of words into a full-scale concert experience. Video design by Nick Dreyden.

Creative Team

  • Video Design: Nick Dreyden
  • Director: Amit Epstein

Season

2026 tour across Israel: Zappa Jerusalem (20.5), Zappa Herzliya (27.5), Reading 3 Tel Aviv (24.6), Music City Be'er Sheva (25.6), Gray Modi'in (4.7), Gray Yehud (25.7) and the North Theatre Haifa (27.8) — with multiple dates sold out.

Visual & Multimedia Concept

Specially for Noam and his team, Nick Dreyden created a unique immersive video design that integrates the poet's lines, brought to life on screen, with his signature video art.
