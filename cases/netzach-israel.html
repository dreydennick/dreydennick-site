NETZACH ISRAEL

Summary

Netzach Israel ("The Eternity of Israel") is a musical-theatrical spectacle that takes the audience on a sweeping journey from the Bible to the Israel of today — through the stories, songs and defining moments that shaped the nation. First-line stars, a live band, actors and dancers perform against full-stage LED scenography: Nick Dreyden, as video designer and LED scenographer, builds the visual world that brings each era to life — one evening in which everything awakens. Produced by Moreshet Theatre in partnership with the Authority for Jewish National Identity at the Prime Minister's Office, with the support of the Ministry of Culture and Sport.
