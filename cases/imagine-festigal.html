IMAGINE FESTIGAL

Summary

Festigal is Israel's largest family entertainment event, a Hanukkah mega-show that combines a musical, pop stars, and TV production. The 2025 edition, "Imagine", celebrated creativity and invention, produced both as a live arena show (EXPO Tel Aviv) and as a television version with specially filmed audition segments.

Nick Dreyden — Guest Video Artist

  • Created the stylish, signature advertising campaign of Imagine Festigal 2025, featuring the production's stars — from Anna Zak to Tal Mosseri and Shahar.
  • Created an original 180° volumetric visual score — immersive panoramic backgrounds and interactive clips designed to wrap around performers.
  • Developed a kinetic video environment for the children's TV auditions: during filming, the studio transformed into virtual worlds, placing each child performer inside a unique multimedia landscape.

Imagine Festigal Merch 2025 — Campaign

Dreyden Visual Atelier served as the production company and creative lead behind the unique advertising campaign for the Imagine Festigal Merch 2025 collection — concept, production and post-production of a series of campaign films (Hoodies, Beauty and Campus) starring the production's stars.

Impact

Dreyden's video score gave the television version of Festigal its distinctive look, elevating the children's auditions into cinematic music-video experiences. His multimedia design was praised for setting a new standard in Festigal's visual language, turning the production into a total multimedia experience.
